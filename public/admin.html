Payment fees used for the estimates

These are estimates for the summary only. Your exact fees are in your Stripe and PayPal reports. Defaults: Stripe standard UK cards 1.5% + 20p; PayPal UK goods & services 2.9% + 30p.

Refunds

Refund a whole order or part of it. Card payments are refunded through Stripe (same keys, so test payments stay test). PayPal and other payments are recorded as manual refunds — no money is moved by the shop. Every refund is saved on the order and in the accounts ledger, and refunds made in your Stripe dashboard are picked up automatically.
