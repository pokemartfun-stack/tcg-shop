FAQ

Quick answers about postage, tracking, payments, card condition and returns.

Postage & delivery

How do you post orders, and how much does it cost?

Every order is sent with Royal Mail Tracked 48. You pay one postage charge per order, based on the largest item in your basket:

  • Large Letter (single cards and card lots): currently £2.90
  • Small Parcel (sealed products, accessories and merch): currently £3.75

The exact postage is always shown in your basket and at checkout before you pay. If we're running a free-postage offer, you'll see it in your basket too.

How long will delivery take?

Royal Mail Tracked 48 aims to deliver within 2–3 working days after we send your order.

Do you post outside the UK?

Not at the moment. We only deliver to addresses in the United Kingdom.

Tracking your order

How do I track my order?

Go to Track order and enter your order number and the email you used at checkout. You'll see your order's progress, and your Royal Mail tracking number once your parcel has been sent.

Payments

How can I pay?

Checkout is handled securely by Stripe. You can pay by debit or credit card, and with Apple Pay or Google Pay on supported devices. Prices are in pounds sterling (GBP).

Do you see or store my card details?

No. You enter your payment details on Stripe's secure page, and we never see or store your full card number.

Do I need an account?

No. Guest checkout is welcome. An account is optional.

Card condition

What do your condition descriptions mean?

Each listing states the card's condition, such as Near Mint. Cards are ungraded unless the listing says otherwise. Condition is our honest assessment, and collectors can judge condition a little differently, so please look at the photos and description, and ask us before you buy if you'd like more detail.

Are the photos of the actual item?

Where a listing has its own photos, they show the actual card or lot you'll receive. Lots and bundles contain the cards described in the listing.

Returns

Can I return something?

Yes. You can cancel your order within 14 days of delivery and return the items for a refund. If something arrives damaged, faulty or not as described, contact us and we'll put it right. See our Terms of Sale.
