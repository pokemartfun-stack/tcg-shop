The data we collect

  • Order details: your name, email address, UK delivery address, phone number (optional), the items you buy, prices and your order number.
  • Payment details: payments are taken by Stripe. We receive confirmation that you've paid and limited details such as the payment status. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Account details (only if you register): your name, email address and your password, which is stored in hashed form.
  • Reviews (only if you leave one): your name, rating, comment and, if you give it, your order number.
  • Marketing choice: whether you ticked "Email me news and offers" at checkout.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email.
  • Technical data: a session cookie that keeps your basket and login working, and standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) kept by our hosting provider for security.

How and why we use it

  • To take, send and support your order, including Royal Mail delivery and tracking (lawful basis: performing our contract with you).
  • To keep business and tax records (lawful basis: legal obligation).
  • To run and protect the shop, prevent fraud and misuse, and show approved customer reviews (lawful basis: our legitimate interests).
  • To send news and offers, only if you opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time (lawful basis: consent).

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the shop and who process it on our behalf:

  • Stripe: payment processing.
  • Render: website hosting.
  • GitHub: secure storage of shop data, such as orders and reviews, in a private repository.
  • Royal Mail: delivery and tracking. We share your name, delivery address and, where needed, contact details.

To show an approximate delivery map on your confirmation and tracking pages, your postcode is looked up with postcodes.io and the map is loaded from OpenStreetMap. We may also share data if the law requires it.

International transfers

Some of our providers (for example Stripe, Render and GitHub) may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is protected by safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

How long we keep it

  • Order and payment records: 6 years from the end of the financial year they relate to, for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Accounts: until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Reviews: while they are shown on the shop, or until you ask us to remove them.
  • Marketing preferences: until you unsubscribe.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie (session) to keep your basket and login working. It expires after 30 days. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout pages to process payments securely and prevent fraud.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your personal data, and to ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or transfer it. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. To use any of these rights, email [CONTACT EMAIL — to confirm]. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
